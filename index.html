Sign Extension and Signed Multiplication

Computer Organization and Architecture - Module 3: Arithmetic Unit

1. Sign Extension

Sign extension makes a two's complement number wider without changing its value. The sign bit is copied into the new leftmost positions: 0s for positive numbers, 1s for negative numbers.

2. Signed Multiplication (Sign-Extension Method)

Both operands are sign-extended to 2n bits. One partial product is formed for each multiplier bit, shifted to its bit position, and all partial products are added. In two's complement the sign bit of the multiplier has negative weight, so its partial product is subtracted.
